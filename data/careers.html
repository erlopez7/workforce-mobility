Risk Model Explainer

What goes into the label. Model 1 blends O*NET-style task shares—how much typical work looks like routine cognitive tasks (repeatable, rule-based desk work), non-routine judgment (planning, novel problems), social or interpersonal coordination, and hands-on or physical duties—with Job Zone (usual preparation depth). The score is a planning lens on automation-style exposure, not a grade on you as a person.

Under each job result we spell out that mix in plain language when the full occupation file loads. If you only see score + zone, open the site over HTTP so data/job_df_full_occupations.csv can load.
