What Is the Opportunity Gap?

The opportunity gap is the difference between what children from low-income and high-income families earn as adults, based on where they grew up.

Data sourced from Opportunity Insights (Raj Chetty et al.), using IRS tax records and US Census data tracking 73,202 neighborhoods.
