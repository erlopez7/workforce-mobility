The Problem

Trapped in Cycles

Low-income students often choose paths with high debt and low earnings because clear guidance is missing.

Data Exists, But It's Inaccessible

Opportunity Atlas, College Scorecard, and labor data are powerful but too complex for most families to use.

No Central Platform

There has not been one place that translates all this data into practical, plain-English support.
